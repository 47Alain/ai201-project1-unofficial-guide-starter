East Campus route changes

Two stops (the MIT Health/Media Lab and the Gray Way stops) have been removed from the Grocery shuttle routes in East Campus as Eversource redirects traffic along Ames Street. Passengers are advised to board at the Kendall Square stop or the Wadsworth Street stop to access the Grocery shuttle routes.

The goal of the grocery shuttles is to provide easier access to more nutritious food options.

Trader Joe's & Whole Foods Market Shuttle

The Division of Student Life sponsors a shuttle to transport MIT students to and from
Trader Joe's
and
Whole Foods Market
with easy access to
Brothers Marketplace
in Kendall Square. The shuttle makes stops at many residences and FSILGs on the Cambridge campus and passes through Kendall Square every Friday and every first, third, and fifth Sunday.

Frequency: This service operates from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM every Friday and every first, third, and fifth Sunday.
